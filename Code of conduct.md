# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for everyone. Participation in this project should be a harassment-free experience regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

By participating in this project, contributors, maintainers, and community members agree to uphold the standards outlined in this Code of Conduct.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project.
- Helping others learn and grow through collaboration.
- Respecting the time and effort of contributors and maintainers.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.
- Offensive, derogatory, or demeaning comments.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior that interferes with productive collaboration.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of facts, contributions, or project information.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Community Standards

To maintain a healthy and productive community:

- Be professional and courteous.
- Assume positive intent.
- Discuss ideas, not individuals.
- Use issue discussions and pull requests constructively.
- Help create an environment where contributors feel comfortable asking questions and sharing ideas.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

When reporting an incident, include:

- A description of the behavior.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or evidence.
- The date and time of the incident, if applicable.
- Any other information that may help maintainers investigate.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially whenever possible.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates these standards.

Possible actions include:

1. A private warning.
2. Temporary restriction of participation.
3. Removal of content, comments, issues, or pull requests.
4. Permanent ban from project participation.

Maintainers will act fairly and consistently when evaluating reported incidents.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Wikis
- Project documentation
- Community events related to the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
